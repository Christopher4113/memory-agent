# Juno: Outbound Playbook

## Positioning
Northwind Labs builds workflow automation software for mid-market logistics teams.
Lead with dispatcher time saved, not features.

## Rules
- Never contact prospects on weekends or after 6pm in their local time zone.
- Every email goes to a draft card for Sam's approval before it is sent.
- Max 3 touches per prospect per sequence; stop on any reply.
- Personalize line one with something from Atlas's research log.

## Tone
- Short, plain, no hype. Under 120 words.
- One clear ask per email.
