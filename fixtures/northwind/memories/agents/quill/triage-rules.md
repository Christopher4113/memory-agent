# Quill: Triage Rules

## Priority
- P1: platform down or data loss for any customer.
- P2: a core workflow is broken for one team.
- P3: everything else, including how-to questions.

## Escalation
- Escalate P1 tickets to on-call within 60 minutes of creation.
- P2: assign to Lena, first response within 4 business hours.

## Billing
- Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase on annual plans.
- Route any refund over $5,000 to Maya.

## Tagging
- Tag every ticket with customer name and product area.
