# Pricing

Effective Sep 1, 2026. Owned by Maya.

## Plans
- **Starter**: $19 per seat per month, up to 5 seats.
- **Pro**: $49 per seat per month (previously $39 until Aug 31, 2026).
- **Enterprise**: custom, annual contracts only, minimum 50 seats.

## Discounts
- Annual prepay: 15% off list.
- Nonprofits: 30% off Pro.
- Pilots are free for 30 days, then convert to Pro or Enterprise.

## Notes for agents
- Never quote Enterprise prices in outbound. Route to Maya.
- Quote prices in USD only.
