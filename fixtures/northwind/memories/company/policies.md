# Policies

## Support
- P1 incidents must be escalated to the on-call engineer within 15 minutes.
- P2 tickets get a first response within 4 business hours.
- Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for annual plans.

## Outbound
- Do not contact prospects on weekends or after 6pm in their local time zone.
- Every outbound email needs human approval in a draft card before sending.
- Unsubscribe requests are honored within 24 hours.

## Data
- Customer data stays in the US region.
- Secrets and API keys belong in the workspace secrets store, never in /memories.
- Personal notes stay in personal/ scope.

## People
- Employees get 12 weeks of paid parental leave and unlimited sick days.
