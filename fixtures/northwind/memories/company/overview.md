# Northwind Labs: Company Overview

Northwind Labs builds workflow automation for mid-market logistics teams.
Founded in 2024 by Maya Chen (CEO) and Dev Patel (CTO). Headquartered in Oakland, CA.

## Mission
Give every dispatcher an assistant that never drops a handoff.

## Products
- **Northwind Core**: shipment exception tracking and routing rules.
- **Northwind v2**: adds agent-driven carrier outreach and a new pricing model.

## Key dates
- v2 launch: Dec 2, 2026
- Series A close target: Q1 2027

## How we work
- Agents (Atlas, Juno, Quill) run 24/7 and write to /memories.
- Humans approve all outbound actions through draft cards.
- Jarvis supports Maya and only writes to her personal scope.
